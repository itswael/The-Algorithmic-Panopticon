## 🎯 Key Takeaways

### The Three Critical Messages

1. **MessageLogger is a Critical Enabler**
   - Ephemeral messaging tools that cache deleted data create vulnerabilities
   - Both SEPB and EFR critically depend on this data
   - **Action:** Disable MessageLogger-style tools for privacy

2. **AI Makes These Threats Real**
   - What required human expertise is now automatable at scale
   - Psychological profiling and forensic reconstruction are technically feasible
   - **Action:** Implement defensive measures now, not later

3. **Ethics Must Guide Technology**
   - Technical capability ≠ ethical acceptability
   - Strong governance and oversight are essential
   - **Action:** Establish ethical frameworks before deployment
