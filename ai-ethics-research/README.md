# AI Ethics Research: Conceptual Models

This directory contains conceptual models for AI systems that could exploit or analyze ephemeral messaging data. These models are created for **educational and ethical research purposes only** to understand potential threats and develop better security measures.

## ⚠️ ETHICAL DISCLAIMER

These conceptual models describe potentially harmful AI applications. They are documented to:
- Raise awareness about privacy vulnerabilities
- Inform defensive security measures
- Guide ethical AI development
- Support academic research in cybersecurity ethics

**DO NOT implement these systems without proper ethical oversight, legal compliance, and institutional review.**

## Contents

1. **Social Engineering Profile Builder** (`social-engineering-profiler.md`)
   - AI system that builds psychological profiles for targeted attacks
   
2. **Ephemeral Forensics Reconstructor** (`ephemeral-forensics-reconstructor.md`)
   - AI-powered forensic tool for reconstructing deleted conversations

3. **Threat Analysis** (`threat-analysis.md`)
   - Comprehensive threat modeling and mitigation strategies

4. **Ethical Considerations** (`ethical-framework.md`)
   - Ethical guidelines and considerations for both offensive and defensive applications

## Research Context

This research examines the intersection of:
- Privacy-focused communication tools (MessageLogger, ephemeral messaging)
- AI/ML capabilities in behavioral analysis
- Digital forensics and data reconstruction
- Social engineering attack vectors
- Defensive security measures
